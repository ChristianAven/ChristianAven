## Selected Cultural Technology Projects

### [OMNIA — Colosseum Archaeological Park](https://visivalab.com/it/portfolio-item/sistema-omnia-catalogazione/)

An integrated heritage platform that centralizes, standardizes, and geolocates the digitized archives of the Colosseum Archaeological Park, supporting research, conservation, and access to complex cultural data.

### [Pompeii Monitoring System](https://visivalab.com/it/portfolio-item/sistema-monitoraggio-pompeii/)

A web and mobile system designed to support field inspections, geolocated data collection, and conservation monitoring across the Archaeological Park of Pompeii, including offline and online synchronization.

### [HERA — Digital Cataloguing System](https://visivalab.com/en/portfolio-item/hera-cataloging-digital-system/)

An integrated platform for cataloguing, managing, and studying the archaeological heritage of Paestum and Velia, connecting records, multimedia content, and geospatial information.

### [HORO — Compasso d'Oro Digital Archive](https://www.adidesignmuseum.org/servizi/archivio-digitale-horo/)

The digital archive of the Compasso d'Oro and ADI, created to preserve and make accessible more than seventy years of Italian design history.

These projects are the result of collaborative work by multidisciplinary teams at Visivalab and its partner institutions. I have also contributed to other digital products and cultural experiences developed by the company.
